==========================================================
                  DARK FOREST ADVENTURE
==========================================================

You went hiking in the big forest, but you walked too far
from the marked path. Now the sun is going down and your
phone has no signal.

The forest is beautiful, but people have not taken good
care of it. There is trash on the ground, a campfire has
been left burning and some animals are in trouble.

YOUR GOAL: find your way to the RANGER STATION.
On the way you can help the forest. The ranger will
notice what you did.
